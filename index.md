Tweakers adds local UI, settings, runtime, and native-bridge extensions to the
OpenAI Codex desktop app.

## Registry

The reviewed machine-readable registry is available at
[`store/index.json`](./store/index.json).

Bundled tweaks use the project-owned `co.tweakers.*` namespace. Public
identifiers describe the Tweakers project rather than an individual
maintainer.
